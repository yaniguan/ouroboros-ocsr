Both numbers use max absolute difference / max absolute reference value. Smaller means closer. These are measured on this image, not dataset accuracy.

ρ(R) rotates space AND permutes orientation channels. At 90° multiples, pixels are permuted exactly. Other angles use interpolation; angles outside C_N have no defined group-action error here.

Maps average absolute values over channels and share one amplitude scale. The reported error uses every channel. A small first-step change does not prove all decoded tokens are unchanged.
